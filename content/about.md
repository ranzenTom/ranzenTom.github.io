I am a Staff Research Scientist and Manager at **InstaDeep (BioNTech Group)**,
where I lead the Genomics AI research program. My work focuses on building large-scale foundation models for
genomics and molecular biology (DNA/RNA), as well as deploying them in real-world biotech settings.

My background is in **applied mathematics and physics**. I trained as an aerospace engineer at Supaero
before transitioning to machine learning. I completed my PhD at Sorbonne University in 2021, focusing on
**deep reinforcement learning** with applications to robotics, collaborating with Google DeepMind and Imperial College London.

I joined InstaDeep in 2018 when they opened their Paris office, and founded the Paris research team in 2020
with a focus on RL and neuroevolution for industrial applications. In 2022, I moved to Boston to open the US office
and build the American research team. Later that year, InstaDeep was acquired by BioNTech for $682M, and I shifted
the team's research focus to foundation models for biology, with an emphasis on genomics.

I am **Head of AI at Bios Life**, based in **Cambridge, Massachusetts**. I joined in **January 2026** to build AI technologies at the intersection of machine learning, biology, and cancer.

My background is in **applied mathematics and physics**. I trained as an aerospace engineer at Supaero and Imperial College London before transitioning to machine learning. I completed my PhD at Sorbonne University in 2021, focusing on **deep reinforcement learning** with applications to robotics, in collaboration with Google DeepMind.

I joined InstaDeep in November 2018 when they opened their Paris office, as one of the first employees, and founded the Paris research team in 2020 with a focus on RL and neuroevolution for industrial applications. In 2022, I moved to Boston to open the US office and build the American research team. InstaDeep was subsequently acquired by BioNTech.

From January 2025 to January 2026, I served as **Head of Genomics AI Research at InstaDeep**, leading the program from New York. My team spanned New York, Paris, and London, developing and deploying foundation models for genomics and transcriptomics across the company.

Our work supported applications at BioNTech and for external customers in industries such as agritech. We open-sourced our research—see the <a href="https://github.com/instadeepai/nucleotide-transformer" target="_blank">GitHub</a> and <a href="https://huggingface.co/InstaDeepAI" target="_blank">HuggingFace</a>—and published in venues including Nature Methods, Nature Machine Intelligence, and leading AI conferences such as NeurIPS.

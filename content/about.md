I am **Head of AI at <a href="https://www.bioslife.ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bios Life</a>**, based in **Cambridge, Massachusetts**, where I lead AI research and its translation into clinical products for personalized cancer screening and surveillance. Our research centers on **foundation models for cancer risk prediction from multimodal clinical data** and **reinforcement learning for personalized screening recommendations**. Alongside this research, we develop **AI capabilities that support clinical care at scale**—transforming large volumes of fragmented medical records into longitudinal patient histories and synthesizing **published scientific evidence and medical guidelines** to inform care decisions.

Previously, I spent seven years at **InstaDeep**, building research teams in Paris and the US before leading **Genomics AI Research**. We developed genomics and transcriptomics foundation models for BioNTech and external partners, with publications in Nature Methods, Nature Machine Intelligence, and NeurIPS, and open-source releases on <a href="https://github.com/instadeepai/nucleotide-transformer" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub</a> and <a href="https://huggingface.co/InstaDeepAI" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hugging Face</a>.

My training spans **applied mathematics, physics, and engineering** at Supaero and Imperial College London. I hold a **PhD from Sorbonne University**, completed in 2021 in collaboration with Google DeepMind, focused on deep reinforcement learning for robotics.

About

I am Head of AI at Bios Life in Cambridge, Massachusetts, leading research and applied AI for personalized cancer screening and surveillance. We develop foundation models for cancer risk prediction from multimodal clinical data and reinforcement learning for screening recommendations. Our clinical AI systems transform fragmented records into longitudinal patient histories and synthesize published scientific evidence and medical guidelines to support care at scale.

Previously, I spent seven years at InstaDeep, building research teams in Paris and the US before leading Genomics AI Research. We developed foundation models for genomics and transcriptomics for BioNTech and external partners. Explore our open-source models and code.

I trained in mathematics, physics, and engineering at Supaero and Imperial College London, then earned my PhD at Sorbonne University in 2021, researching deep reinforcement learning in collaboration with Google DeepMind.

About

I am a Staff Research Scientist and Manager at InstaDeep (BioNTech Group), where I lead the Genomics AI research program. My work focuses on building large-scale foundation models for genomics and molecular biology (DNA/RNA), as well as deploying them in real-world biotech settings.

My broader research interests include Deep Reinforcement Learning, Large Language Models, and Neuroevolution. I previously completed my PhD at Sorbonne University, focusing on sample efficiency in Deep RL, and collaborated with Google DeepMind and Imperial College London.
